NEURAL IMPLEMENTATION METHODOLOGY

How The AI Detection System Works

Every submitted video is processed through an automated 8-stage forensic pipeline producing frame-level evidence and risk certification.

STAGE 01

Secure Account

Role-based authentication with optional 2FA verification for investigative audit tracking.

STAGE 02

Video Ingestion

Drag-and-drop submission supporting MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV up to 50 MB with SHA-256 integrity validation.

STAGE 03

Extract Frames

OpenCV engine executes uniform stride sampling across video timecodes preserving temporal continuity.

STAGE 04

Face Crop & Align

OpenCV face detection finds the face in each sampled frame and crops it to a 224×224 RGB image.

STAGE 05

EfficientNet-B0

Deep CNN extracts 1280-dim feature vectors identifying blending borders, GAN texture residues, and warping.

STAGE 06

BiLSTM Modeling

Bidirectional recurrent units analyze frame-to-frame physiological consistency (eye blinks, head movement).

STAGE 07

Risk Scoring

A sigmoid output gives the overall fake probability, mapped to Low (<35%), Medium (35–60%) or High (≥60%) risk.

STAGE 08

Forensic Report

Generates explainable keyframe telemetry, timeline anomaly curve, and downloadable audit PDF dossier.
