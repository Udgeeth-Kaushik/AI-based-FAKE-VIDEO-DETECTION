STEP-BY-STEP FORENSIC VERIFICATION

How The Detection Engine Works

From raw digital video streams to explainable forensic certificates: an inside look at our 8-stage automated spatio-temporal deep-learning pipeline.

01

Secure Authentication & Access

PORTAL ENTRANCE

Users authenticate through secure role-based portals with optional multi-factor authentication (TOTP 2FA). Every verification session establishes a tamper-proof session token ensuring forensic chain-of-custody tracking.

02

Multi-Format Video Ingestion

MP4 / AVI / MOV / MKV ≤ 50MB

The video is validated on client and server levels. Length (up to 60 seconds) and size limits are checked, then evenly spaced frames are sampled from the video.

03

Uniform Stride Frame Sampling

OPENCV DECIMATION

Hardware-accelerated OpenCV decoders sample keyframes using uniform temporal strides. By retaining chronological sequences rather than randomized images, the system preserves motion dynamics required for subsequent temporal RNN analysis.

04

Face Detection & Crop

224×224 NORMALIZATION

An OpenCV Haar cascade detector finds the face in each sampled frame. Faces are cropped and resized to 224×224 RGB images. Frames with no face are skipped and counted separately.

05

EfficientNet-B0 Spatial Feature Extraction

1,280-DIM EMBEDDINGS

Each cropped face tensor passes through EfficientNet-B0. The compound-scaled network inspects high-frequency spectral residuals, blending discrepancies along facial borders, and subtle GAN color distortions, generating a rich 1,280-dimensional feature embedding per frame.

06

Bidirectional LSTM Temporal Analysis

256 UNITS • 16 FACE FRAMES

The sequence of 1,280-dim vectors is fed into a 256-unit BiLSTM. Looking at past and future frames together lets the network pick up flicker and inconsistencies across frames. Every frame also gets its own score, which is how suspicious frames are listed.

07

Sigmoid Output & Risk Scoring

PROBABILITY % • 3 TIERS

The model outputs a fake probability from 0% to 100%. The system labels it Low Risk (<35%), Medium Risk (35–60%) or High Risk (≥60%). A frame is called suspicious when its own score is 50% or more.

08

Forensic Audit Dossier & Certification

EXPLAINABLE REPORT

The final output compiles the keyframe telemetry breakdown table, timeline anomaly curves, suspicious frame zoom gallery, and generates an official downloadable forensic audit PDF saved into the user's historical audit database.
